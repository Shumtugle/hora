Voice sample 8

One short sample, 7.6 seconds, used as a voice reference sample for the
eighth voice. It stands with the other voices.

Origin. The sample is synthetic speech, made by the author of this app from
a short text of his own; the recording matches its text letter for letter.
Which speech synthesizer made it was not recorded, so no licence of that
synthesizer is claimed here. No claim is made beyond this account.

Changes: the sample was cut at a pause after its third line, trimmed with
short fades, and its peak set to a common level. Nothing else was altered.

The app has no voice cloning, and this sample must not be used to
impersonate people, to mislead, or for any harmful or unlawful purpose.
Chapters of audiobooks carry a note that their speech is synthetic.
