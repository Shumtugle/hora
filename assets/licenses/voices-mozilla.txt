Voice samples 4 and 5

Short excerpts from the single-speaker Russian speech datasets "Denis 1.0" (sample 4) and
"Dmitri 1.0" (sample 5) published on Mozilla Data Collective, dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Sample 4 is not the excerpt itself but a sentence read by Hora's own speech model with that
excerpt as its voice: of eight readings of one calm sentence, the evenest that kept the pitch
of the original was taken. The voice stays the speaker's; only the manner is calmer.
