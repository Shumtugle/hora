Edited editions

Books found under "Edited editions" in "Find a free book" are put together from
Standard Ebooks, a volunteer project that proofreads books whose rights have run
out against scans of their printed editions. The texts and the paintings on their
covers are believed to be in the public domain in the United States; they may
still be under copyright in other countries, so check the law where you live.
The project's own work on them (markup, corrections, cover layout) is dedicated
to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each book keeps its title,
author and cover; its own pages name its sources and everyone who worked on it.
