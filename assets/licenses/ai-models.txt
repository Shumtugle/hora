AI model (downloaded separately, not part of the app)

The model for talk is Gemma 4 E2B instruct by Google, in the GGUF format,
quantized to 4 bits. The part for photos (vision projector) and the accelerator
(a small drafting model) belong to the same model.

All three files are distributed under the Apache License 2.0, the licence of the
model they come from; the full text is in apache-2.0.txt and is also published
beside the files in the app's download release.

Hora checks every downloaded file against the checksum of its original source.
A model file chosen by the user is not checked: Hora cannot know what is inside it.

The model answers in words only. It has no tools and takes no actions: looking up
the reference, the weather or the book is done by Hora itself, on the user's request.
