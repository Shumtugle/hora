English voice module (downloaded separately, not part of the app)

The English speech model is Pocket TTS by Kyutai Labs, licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Pocket TTS was created by
Manu Orsini, Simon Rouard, Gabriel De Marmiesse, Vaclav Volhejn, Neil Zeghidour
and Alexandre Defossez.

Changes: the model was exported to ONNX and partly quantized to 8-bit integers
for on-device use; the input and output types of two of its graphs were widened
from 16-bit to 32-bit floats. The weights were not retrained.

The same five built-in voices speak through it; there is no voice cloning.
