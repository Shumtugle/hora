The catalogue of news feeds by rubric (assets/talk/news-catalog.txt) is taken from
Kite by Kagi Search, github.com/kagisearch/kite-public, file kite_feeds.json: only the
rubrics in Russian and English, only the feeds asked for over https. The feeds
themselves belong to their publishers. Hora shows their headlines only, never the
stories, and reads a headline aloud only when it is touched.
