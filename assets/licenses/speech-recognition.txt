Speech recognition

The microphone is heard by a small streaming Zipformer model for Russian,
vosk-model-small-streaming-ru by Alpha Cephei, licensed under the Apache
License 2.0; the full text is in apache-2.0.txt. The model was converted for
sherpa-onnx by the Next-gen Kaldi team (k2-fsa); its 8-bit version is used as
published, unchanged.

It runs on the phone itself: no sound leaves it, and nothing heard is kept.
