Voice samples 6 to 9 and 13 to 15

Seven short samples, one per voice (6 female, 7 male, 8 male, 9 female,
13 female, 14 female, 15 male), used as voice reference samples. Voices 6, 7
and 9 stand with the other voices; voices 8 and 13 to 15 appear only while the
developer room of the app is open.

Origin. Each sample is cut from speech generated (samples 6 to 9 on 11 October,
samples 13 to 15 on 12 October 2026) by the
voice design model Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign (Qwen team, Alibaba Cloud),
https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign
The speech was made in the model's official public demo,
https://huggingface.co/spaces/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS
which runs the same open weights (no separate cloud service) and is marked
Apache 2.0. Each voice was designed from a written description of a voice.
No recording of any person was used as input: these voices are not clones
of a real person and do not imitate one. The text read is a short passage
of Russian prose written for this app; only Russian sentences were kept.

Licence. The model and its code are licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 (Copyright 2026 Alibaba Cloud); the full text is in the notice
"Apache License 2.0" of this app. The licence covers the model; it states
no conditions for audio the model generates, and no other terms are shown
by the model card or the demo. The Qwen team has not given a separate
written confirmation for generated voices used inside an app.

Changes: each sample was cut to whole sentences, trimmed at the edges with
short fades, and its level set to a common peak; in sample 14 long pauses
between sentences were shortened, and samples 13 and 15 were slowed (by a
fifth and a quarter, pitch kept) so the voices read at a calmer pace.
Nothing else was altered.

The app has no voice cloning, and these samples must not be used to
impersonate people, to mislead, or for any harmful or unlawful purpose.
Chapters of audiobooks carry a note that their speech is synthetic.
