Books from the free library

Books found in Wikisource through "Find a free book" are put together from Wikisource, the free
library. Its works are in the public domain, or were given to everyone by their
translators under a free license; the library's own text is available under the
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each book keeps the work's title,
author and language; the work's page in the library names its source and license.
