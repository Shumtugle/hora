Voice model

The speech model is the Russian model Shtorm_PocketTTS_RU v1 by ArtShtorm,
licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
https://huggingface.co/ArtShtorm/Shtorm_PocketTTS_RU

It is built on Pocket TTS by Kyutai Labs, licensed CC BY 4.0.
Pocket TTS was created by Manu Orsini, Simon Rouard, Gabriel De Marmiesse,
Vaclav Volhejn, Neil Zeghidour and Alexandre Defossez.
https://github.com/kyutai-labs/pocket-tts

Changes: the model was exported to ONNX and partly quantized to 8-bit integers
for on-device use; the input and output types of two of its graphs were widened
from 16-bit to 32-bit floats. The weights were not retrained. The full-precision
audio decoder offered as an option is Kyutai's own, taken from the English
Pocket TTS export and widened the same way.

The app has no voice cloning: it speaks only with its five built-in samples, whose
origin is described in the notices for the voice samples. The model must not be
used to impersonate people, to mislead, or for any harmful or unlawful purpose.
Chapters of audiobooks carry a note that their speech is synthetic.
